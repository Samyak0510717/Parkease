Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about parking with ParkEase Nepal.

What happens if I arrive 10-15 minutes late?

Your reserved bay is held exclusively for your booking window with a 20-minute grace period from your reservation start time.

How do I settle my parking payment?

You can pay online using Khalti digital wallet during booking, or generate a reservation pass and settle via cash or counter QR at the parking gate.

How do I extend my parking if I need more time?

Simply log in to your ParkEase Driver Account from your mobile browser, locate your active booking, and click "Extend Time".

Are electric vehicle (EV) charging ports available?

New Road Multi-Level and Baneshwor Hub both feature dedicated 7.4kW Type-2 AC chargers available for four-wheeler bookings.
